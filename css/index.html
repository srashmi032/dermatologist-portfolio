Before Your Visit

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens at my first visit?

Dr. Gupta will ask about your skin concerns and history, examine your skin, and run an AI skin scan if it helps. You will leave with a treatment plan and an estimate before any treatment begins.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your concern and skin type. Pigmentation and scars usually need a series of sessions. Dr. Gupta will give you an estimate at your consultation.

Is there downtime after laser or MNRF treatment?

Pico Laser usually has minimal downtime. MNRF can cause mild redness for a day or two. You will be told what to expect for your skin, and given aftercare instructions, before you leave.

How much do treatments cost?

Cost depends on the treatment and the number of sessions. You will get a clear estimate after your consultation.
